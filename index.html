Model & limitations

Synthetic height maps, 1 m raster, circular rover, four-direction grid motion. Rocks use swept-circle collision checks. Slope and terrain relief are checked over a conservative swept raster box.

Score = step distance x [1 + 3(slope / limit)� + relief / clearance]. It is a dimensionless-weighted distance proxy, not measured energy or failure probability.

Distance A* minimizes grid distance. Terrain A* minimizes score. Quick mode uses weighted A* (weight 2), which can trade route quality for search effort; it is not guaranteed faster. Scores share the same safety limits. A distance candidate over the score limit is rejected; this does not prove no acceptable alternative exists.

The initial map lacks small depressions. An idealized 4 m local sensor reveals them. A simulator truth guard stops before a missed geometric hazard. No soil slip, stuck-wheel dynamics, camera uncertainty or real Mars data is modelled. This is not ACE or flight AutoNav.
